Scoring

How role readiness is worked out

Each role has a set of skills with different importance weights. Your level on each skill contributes to the total.

Readiness = skill weight × your level, added together

Learning = 35% of a skill’s weight. Comfortable = 70%. Confident = 100%. The score is a guide for prioritizing learning, not a hiring prediction.

Scores are calculated in your browser from a sample role catalogue.
